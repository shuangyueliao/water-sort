# Water Sort · Liquid Sorting Puzzle

A free, single-file HTML5 puzzle game. Tap a glass tube to lift it, tap
another to pour — liquids only stack on the same color or an empty tube.
Unite every color to cap the tube and clear the level. 40 procedurally
generated, solver-verified levels, unlimited undo, a once-per-level
"+1 tube" booster, and star ratings. No dependencies, no build step,
no tracking — one `index.html`.

**Play:** https://shuangyueliao.github.io/water-sort/

- Mobile touch + desktop mouse (keyboard: arrows move the cursor,
  Enter selects/pours, Z/U undo, R restart, M mute, Esc level select)
- Procedural graphics & WebAudio sounds (pour gurgle, cap clink,
  win fanfare) — zero external assets
- Progress, best scores, stars and sound preference saved in localStorage

## Levels

All 40 levels are generated by the reverse-move method (starting from the
solved state, random legal-shaped pours scramble the board) and then
verified by a built-in DFS solver: every shipped board has at least one
legal solution, and par is guaranteed reachable, so 3 stars (finish at or
under par) is always attainable. Difficulty ramps from 3 colors / 5 tubes
to 8 colors / 10 tubes.

## Submitting to portals

The game is a single self-contained HTML file, ready for HTML5 game portals
(CrazyGames, GameDistribution, itch.io). Fork/clone and submit. Platform SDKs
are loaded only on their own domains (domain-guarded loader); standalone and
GitHub Pages runs load nothing external.
