# Water Sort — Store Submission Materials

## Short description (CrazyGames / GameDistribution / itch.io)

Sort colored liquids between glass tubes until every color unites. 40
solver-verified levels, unlimited undo, a tube booster, relaxing pours and
satisfying caps. Pure logic, zero clutter.

(188 characters)

## Details

- Genre: Puzzle / Brain / Casual (water sort, ball-sort style)
- Controls: Mouse / Touch — tap a tube to lift it, tap another to pour.
  Keyboard: arrows move a cursor, Enter selects/pours, Z/U undo, R restart,
  M mute, Esc back to levels.
- Features: 40 progressively harder levels (3→8 colors, 5→10 tubes),
  unlimited undo, once-per-level "+1 tube" booster, star ratings by moves
  vs par, best-score saving, unlimited restarts, procedural graphics &
  synthesized sounds, single-file HTML5 (no external assets), works offline
  once loaded.
- Every level is generated by the reverse-move method and verified by a
  built-in solver plus an independent BFS in testing — always solvable, par
  always reachable for 3 stars.
- Tech: Vanilla JavaScript, Canvas, WebAudio. No frameworks, no tracking,
  no external links inside gameplay.
